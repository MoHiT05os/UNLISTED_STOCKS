2018

The Origins

Back in 2018, the unlisted space was largely opaque. Pricing was discovered through personal WhatsApp networks, and retail investors had no reliable way to verify broker quotes or research financials of pre-IPO companies.

We started SECRET LIST as a simple blog to share indicative prices and basic research reports on upcoming IPOs and private companies, aiming to bring light to this dark market.

2021

What we Built

As retail interest in startups surged, so did the traffic to SECRET LIST. We scaled our operations, bringing in full-time SEBI-registered research analysts to provide deep dives into company DRHPs, annual reports, and unit economics.

We launched the first comprehensive Screener for unlisted stocks, allowing investors to filter companies by sector, revenue, and valuation multiples.

Today

The Future

Today, SECRET LIST is India's most trusted information platform for the private market, serving over 1,00,000 investors and tracking 255+ companies daily.

We continue to innovate with features like Live Price Alerts, ESOP monetization tools, and comprehensive Video Research, making the journey from unlisted to IPO seamless for our users.
